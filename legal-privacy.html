Privacy Policy

SecretGen generates random secrets. It doesn't ask you to sign up, log in, or hand over any personal information.

What we collect

Standard web server logs (IP address, requested path, user agent, timestamp) for operating and securing the service. These logs are not sold, shared, or used for tracking, and are rotated out periodically.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no analytics trackers, no advertising identifiers, no cookies (see the Cookies Policy). Generated secrets are not logged or stored anywhere - they exist only in the response sent back to you.

Third parties

This API runs on Vercel's serverless infrastructure. No other third-party services see your requests except whatever network sits between you and Vercel (your ISP, any CDN in front of it).

Questions about any of this? Contact legal@stuxapis.net.
