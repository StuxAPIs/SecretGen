Terms and Ethics

SecretGen is a free, public service that returns randomly generated secrets. By using it, you agree to the following:

Fair use

Don't hammer the API with excessive automated requests. Reasonable personal and hobby-project use is fine; if your traffic looks like it's trying to take the service down, we'll rate-limit or block it.

No warranty

The API is provided "as is", with no uptime guarantee. It's a free side project, not a paid SLA-backed service.

Your responsibility

Secrets returned by this API are only as strong as the length you request, and are transmitted over whatever connection you make the request with. Using them securely (storing, transmitting, and rotating them appropriately) is your responsibility.

Questions about any of this? Contact legal@stuxapis.net.
